2.15.1.1 Table: Seeding Principles for Specific Categories of Tournaments
For Division I, Junior and Cadet National Tournaments, seeding order based on rankings will be
determined by the rankings using the Unified Points Lists, according to the Trial points structure.
Rankings can be found in the Membership Database by clicking here. The Team Selection points
lists will NOT be used in tournament seeding.
