## Where do fencers restart?

Restart position depends on a scored touch, period start, ground held, contact or passing; correct fencing distance can affect a rear-line result.

Facts to establish: Was a touch awarded? Why was fencing stopped? Was one foot already behind the rear line?

Draft retrieval terms: restart, on guard, distance, moved back, reset position.

## Touch before Fence or after Halt

A new action cannot start after Halt; a movement already begun can remain valid subject to the specific stopping rule.

Facts to establish: When did the action start? Which stopping condition applied?

Draft retrieval terms: late hit, after stop, before fence.

## Touch as time expires

Expiration of time is a distinct stop: linked clock preserves already registered touches; an unlinked timekeeper stop does not allow a launched hit to continue.

Facts to establish: Was the clock connected to the apparatus? Was registration before cutoff? Was wireless apparatus used?

Draft retrieval terms: buzzer, zero seconds, after time, last second, time expired.
