        The FIE gold medal, which is the Grand Prix des Nations, is awarded each year as follows:
        Grand Prix des nations Cadets
        Grand Prix des nations Juniors
        Grand Prix des nations Seniors
        Grand Prix des nations Veterans

        In each of the these categories, the Grand Prix des nations will be awarded to the nation which
        has won the most gold medals in all the individual and team events of the corresponding World
        Championships.

        If two nations have equal scores, the winner will be the one which has won the most silver
        medals.

        If there is still equality, the winner will be the one which has won the most bronze medals.
        If there is still equality, both nations are declared winners, and both will receive the Grand Prix
        des nations.
