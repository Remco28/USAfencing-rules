o.42
  1.   The Zonal Championships recognised by the FIE are the Senior Zonal Championships and the
       Junior Zonal Championships. Other competitions may be organized by the Zonal Confederations
       (Veterans, U 23, Cadet circuit, etc.).

  2.   The program of the Senior Zonal Championships comprises twelve events, six individual and six
       team —men’s foil, women’s foil, men’s epee, women’s epee, men’s sabre and women’s sabre.

  3.   The program of the Junior Zonal Championships comprises 6 individual events, and 6 team
       events — men’s foil, women’s foil, men’s epee, women’s epee, men’s sabre and women’s sabre.

  4.   The formulae of the competitions are specified in the rules and in the following articles: o.100,
       o.101 and o.102 (o.102.1 first sentence and o.102.2).

  5.   For Senior and Junior Individual Zone Championships, at each weapon, the federations may
       enter up to four fencers. For Senior and Junior Team Zone Championships, at each weapon,
       each federation may enter 1 team (consisting of three fencers with or without a reserve).
