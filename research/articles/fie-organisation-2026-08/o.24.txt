  1    Refereeing at World Championships is carried out by referees chosen by the Executive
       Committee of the FIE at the proposal of the Refereeing Commission.

  2    Referees’ travel and board and lodging costs are paid by the Organising Committee, who in
       return will receive all the entry fees.
  3    Referees must attend the refereeing meeting that takes place the day before the World
       Championships.
