       The programme of the Junior and Cadet World Fencing Championships comprises twelve
       individual events (six junior and six cadet), and six junior team events — at male foil, female foil,
       male épée, female épée, male sabre and female sabre. At each weapon, male and female, the
       World Championships begin with the individual junior events, followed by the individual cadet
       events and lastly the junior team events.
