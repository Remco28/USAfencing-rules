  1    The number of FIE A or B Grade referees that must accompany delegations to cadet and junior
       World Cup competitions and satellite competitions is:

       1–4 fencers:           No obligation to provide a referee
       5–9 fencers:           One referee
       10 or more fencers:    Two referees
       1 Junior Team:         One referee

       For cadet and junior World Cup competitions and satellite competitions, the name(s) of the
       referee(s) (who must have an FIE category in the weapon of the competition for which they are
       entered) must be entered on the FIE website 7 days before the competition (midnight, Lausanne
       time).

  2    Should a national Federation not provide the required number of referees, a fine (cf o.31,
       table of financial penalties and fines) will be inflicted on it.
  3    In addition, the FIE Supervisor of cadet and junior World Cup competitions is required to report on
       gender diversity of the registered referees through the FIE Supervisor’s report.
