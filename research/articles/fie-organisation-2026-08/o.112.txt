o.112

Appointment of refereeing, SEMI and medical delegates

Two Refereeing delegates, one SEMI delegate and three (3) Medical delegates must be the members of
the FIE Commissions and be appointed by the FIE Executive Committee following the recommendations of
the respective commissions.
For the Veteran World Championships, of the six (6) designated delegates there must be at least two of
each gender, if the availability of the delegates and continental representation allow it.

Application: immediate.
