  1     From the beginning of the direct elimination tableau, if for whatever reason, a fencer does not
        fence, or is unable to fence, or does not complete his bout, his opponent is declared winner of
        that bout. A fencer who withdraws does not lose his place in the overall classification of the
        competition and is recorded in the result list with the information “Did Not Finish”.

  2     A fencer who is excluded is scratched from the direct elimination table and his results are
        annulled as if he had not taken part. The fencer is recorded without a classification at the end of
        the final classification list with the information “Excluded”. Cf. t.149.2, t.170.
