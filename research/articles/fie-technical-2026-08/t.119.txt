t.119
          During a competition, individual or team, if a fencer does not present himself on the piste,
          ready to fence, when ordered to do so by the Referee:
          a. The fencer or team member not present will be penalised with a Yellow Card;
          b. A second call will be made, one minute after the first call, followed by a Red Card for the
          fencer or team member not present;
          c. A third and last call will be made, one minute after the second call, followed by elimination
          from the competition for the fencer not present in an individual competition or for the whole
          team in a team competition.
