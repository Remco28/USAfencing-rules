t.159
          Penalties are cumulative and they are valid for the bout with the exception of those indicated
          by a BLACK CARD, which means exclusion from the competition, suspension for the remainder
          of the tournament and for the following 60 days of the active season (1 September – World
          Championships for the Cadets and Juniors, and 1 September – World Championships for the
          Seniors), whether current or forthcoming, or both (cf.t.169.2).
