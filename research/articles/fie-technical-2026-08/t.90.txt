t.90
      1    The épée is a thrusting weapon only. Attacks with this weapon are therefore only with the point.

      2    Pushing or letting the point of the electric weapon drag on the electric piste is forbidden during
           the actual bout (between ‘Play!’ and ‘Halt!’). Placing the weapon on the piste at any time to
           straighten it is also forbidden.

           Any breaking of this rule will be penalised according to Articles t.158-162, t.165, t.170, for the
           offences of the 1st group.
