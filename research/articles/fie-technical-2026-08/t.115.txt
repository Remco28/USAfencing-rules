t.115
      1   When presenting themselves to fence a bout, the fencers must arrive on the piste completely
          ready to fence — regulation clothing, jacket fastened, sword-hand gloved and holding the
          weapon, body wire connected to the plug inside the guard. The mask should be carried in the
          unarmed hand.

      2   Before the start of the bout, the fencers' hair must be fastened and placed inside the clothing
          and/or mask in such a way as to ensure that:

          - it does not cover a valid surface (and thus prevent a hit from being scored).
          - it does not conceal the name and nationality of the fencer.
          - it does not need to be put back in place during the bout, thus interrupting it.
          In the case of violation of this rule, the referee will apply the penalties for group 1 offences
          (t.158- 162, t.165, t.170).
