t.27

      1   Displacing the target and ducking are allowed even if during the action the unarmed hand
          and/or the knee of the rear leg comes into contact with the piste.

      2   It is forbidden to turn one’s back on one’s opponent during the bout. Should such an offence
          occur, the Referee will penalise the fencer at fault as specified in articles t.158-162, t.165,
          t.170, for the offences of the 1st group and any hit scored by the fencer at fault is annulled.
