t.33
      1    When a competitor crosses one of the lateral boundaries of the piste with one or both feet
           completely off the piste, the Referee must immediately call ‘Halt!’
      2    If the fencer goes off the piste with both feet, the Referee must annul everything that has
           occurred after the boundary has been crossed, except a hit received by the competitor who has
           crossed the boundary even after he has crossed it, provided that this hit results from a simple
           and immediate action.

      3    However, a hit scored by the fencer who leaves the piste with one foot only is valid provided
           that the action was started before the ‘Halt!’.

      4    If one of the competitors leaves the piste with both feet, only a hit made by the fencer who
           remains on the piste with at least one foot, and at epee only, can be counted valid, even in the
           case of a double hit. For foil and sabre the convention must be applied.
