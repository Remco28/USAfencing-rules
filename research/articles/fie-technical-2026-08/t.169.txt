      1   The first infringement in the Fourth Group, is penalised by a BLACK CARD (exclusion from
          the competition, suspension from the remainder of the tournament and for the following 60 days
          of the active season (1 September – World Championships for the Cadets and Juniors, and 1
          September – World Championships for the Seniors), whether current or forthcoming or both).


      2   Furthermore any black card awarded at a competition of the FIE or at a competition organized by
          any Confederation which has subscribed to the FIE disciplinary code shall be reported within 10
          days to the President of the FIE, for him to assess whether the severity of the offence committed
          warrants the sending of the report made by the FIE supervisor or by the Directoire Technique to
          the president of the Legal Commission, requesting him to establish a Disciplinary Tribunal to
          determine if penalties in addition to those imposed at the competition should be imposed.
