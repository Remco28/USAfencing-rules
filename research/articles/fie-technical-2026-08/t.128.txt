t.128
         Competitors must fence to their utmost ability in a sportsmanlike manner until the end of the
         competition in order to obtain the best possible classification, without giving away hits or
         seeking to be favoured by being given hits by anyone (cf. t.158-162, t.169, t.170). Any
         infringement will be sanctioned for the offences of the 4th group.


         The team manager
