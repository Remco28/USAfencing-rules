t.43
      1      The fencers may ask how much time they have left to fence each time that the fencing is
             interrupted if the time is not provided on the apparatus.

      2      Any fencer who attempts improperly to cause or to prolong interruptions to the bout is
             penalised as specified in Articles t.158-162, t.165, t.170, for the offences of the 1st group.
