         At the end of a bout, the Referee must bring together the two fencers, to announce clearly the
         score, which will be transmitted to the Directoire Technique. He must say clearly: ‘Mister X won
         against Mister Y with the following score…’

         At the end of a team match, the Referee must bring together the two teams, to
         announce clearly the score, which will be transmitted to the Directoire Technique.
         He must say clearly: ‘Team X won against Team Y with the following score…’

         Fencers must present in either national uniform (m.25) or national tracksuit. Fencers must not
         present on piste in a state of undress.
