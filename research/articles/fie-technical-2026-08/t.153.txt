t.153
      1   Disqualification of a competitor does not necessarily incur his (temporary) suspension or
          permanent suspension, if he has acted in good faith; a request for supplementary penalties for
          fraudulent intention may, however, be made against the competitor.

      2   A team which has included a competitor who is disqualified necessarily incurs the penalty
          of that member and is also disqualified.

      3   The consequences of disqualification are the same as those for exclusion from the competition
          (see Article t.149).
