t.66
  1     For bouts in the direct elimination and the finals at the World Championships and the
        Olympic Games, and for finals of World Cup and Grand Prix competitions, the two fencers
        in each bout will go to the equipment checking zone, located near the piste, 30 minutes before
        they are due on the piste. Their equipment will be checked under the responsibility of the SEMI
        (or the designated expert for finals of World Cup competitions). If any anomaly is identified
        the equipment at fault will be changed at once, without any penalty being applied. The SEMI
        delegate will hand over the bodywires, the masks and the weapons that have been checked to
        the Referee for the bout. Ten minutes before they are due on the piste the fencers will report
        to the Referee designated for their bout. The Referee will hand over a bodywire to each of the
        fencers in the access zone for the piste. He will check that the fencers are wearing regulation
        under-jacket protection.

  2     The Referee and the fencers must stay together in the access zone until they go on the piste.
        One minute before they go on the piste the Referee will give a weapon to each fencer, for him
        to plug in his bodywire. No check will be carried out on the piste prior to the bout.
