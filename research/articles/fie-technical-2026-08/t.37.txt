      1   Duration of the bout is held to mean the effective duration, that is the total of the intervals of
          time between the orders ‘Play!’ and ‘Halt!’.

      2    The duration of the bout is controlled by the Referee or by a time-keeper. For the finals of all
           official competitions, as well as for all bouts for which a chronometer is visible to the spectators,
           the chronometer must be so placed that it is visible to the two fencers on the piste and to the
           Referee.
