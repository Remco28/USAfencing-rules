t.114
          The fencers, completely equipped, with all equipment conforming with the regulations (cf. t.64–
          t.70) and ready to fence, must be present at the time and place appointed for the beginning
          of the pool, match, or bout of direct elimination, or at the time appointed for the checking of their
          equipment before their bout (cf. t.64-66), as well as during the competition, whenever the
          Referee requires it.
