t.148
          A competitor may have a hit awarded against him which he has not in fact received, either
          because he has crossed the rear limit of the piste (cf. t.34), or because he has committed
          an offence which has prevented his opponent fencing (a flèche attack which jostles the
          opponent, the use of the unarmed hand while fencing, etc.) according to the Technical Rules.

          Exclusion
