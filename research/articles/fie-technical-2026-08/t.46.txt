t.46
      1      By accepting a position as referee or judge, the person so designated pledges his honour to
             respect the Rules and to cause them to be respected, and to carry out his duties with the
             strictest impartiality and absolute concentration.

      2      Referees may not combine their function with any other activity during the tournament,
             such as member of the Directoire Technique, team captain, official delegate of their national
             federation, trainer, etc.
