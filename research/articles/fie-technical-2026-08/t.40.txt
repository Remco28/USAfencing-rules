t.40

      1    The bout ends when:
      -    One of the fencers has scored 15 hits; or
      -    9 minutes of effective fencing time have passed.

      2    The fencer who has scored the greater number of hits is declared the winner.

      3    If at the end of regulation time the scores are equal, the fencers fence for a deciding hit, with
           a maximum time limit of one minute. Before the fencing recommences the Referee draws lots to
           decide who will be the winner if scores are still equal at the end of the extra minute.

      4    In this case the score recorded on the score-sheet is the real score achieved in the bout.
