t.113
      1   No fencer (individual or team) from an FIE member national federation may take part in an
          official competition if he refuses to fence against any other fencer whatsoever (individual or
          team) correctly entered in the event. Should this rule be broken, the penalties specified for
          offences of the 4th group will be applied (cf. t.158-162, t.169, t.170).
      2   The FIE shall consider whether there are grounds, and to what extent, for taking sanctions
          against the national federation to which the disqualified competitor belongs (cf. FIE Statutes
          1.2.4 and Rules Article t.170).
