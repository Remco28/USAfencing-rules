t.164
          The offences and their penalties which appear in different articles of the Rules are
          summarised in the table that follows in Article t.170; they are divided into four groups (cf. t.165–
          t.169). All these penalties are within the competence of the Referee, although the Directoire
          Technique still retains the right to intervene on its own initiative (cf. t.139.1–3).
