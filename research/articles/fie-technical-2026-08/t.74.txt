t.74
               For all official FIE competitions, every fencer must appear on the piste with clothing
               conforming to the rules as follows:

           1   Name and nationality, in accordance with the rules, on the back of the jacket.

           2   Wearing his national uniform and logo (cf. m.25.3).

               In case of violation of this rule, the referee will eliminate the fencer at fault from the event in
               question.
