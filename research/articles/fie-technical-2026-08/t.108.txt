t.108
      1   Fencers must observe strictly and faithfully the Rules and the Statutes of the FIE, the
          particular rules for the competition in which they are engaged, the traditional customs of courtesy
          and integrity and the instructions of the officials.

      2   In particular they will subscribe, in an orderly, disciplined and sporting manner, to the
          following provisions; all breaches of these rules may entail punishments by the competent
          disciplinary authorities after, or even without, prior warning, according to the facts and
          circumstances (cf. t.158–t.170).
