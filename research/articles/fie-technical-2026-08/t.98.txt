               A hit which arrives on a non-valid part of the target is not counted as a hit; it is not registered
               by the apparatus, it does not stop the fencing phrase and does not annul any subsequent hits.
               However, a hit which arrives following a fencing fault or after going over the lateral boundary of the
               piste with both feet is not counted as a valid hit, but it stops the fencing phrase and therefore
               annuls any subsequent hit.
