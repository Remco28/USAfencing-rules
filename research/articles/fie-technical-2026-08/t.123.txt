t.123
          During or after a bout, even if the fencer has already left the piste, any act against the spirit of
          sportsmanship such as violently or dangerously throwing one’s mask or any other piece of
          equipment will be penalised as specified in article t.169 (c.f. t.108.1, 2 and t.109), for the
          offences of the 4th group.


          Unwillingness to fight (Non-combativity)
