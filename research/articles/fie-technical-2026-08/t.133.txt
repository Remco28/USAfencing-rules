t.133
          Spectators are obliged not to interfere with the good order of a competition, to do nothing
          which may tend to influence the fencers or the Referee, and to respect the decisions of the latter
          even when they do not agree with them. They must obey any instructions which the Referee
          may deem it necessary to give them (cf. t.109-110, t.167-168, t.170, penalties for the offences
          of the 3rd group).
