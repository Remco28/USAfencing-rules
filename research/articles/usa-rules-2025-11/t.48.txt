USA Fencing Rules for Competition                      20                                Version: November 2025
       1.   The Referee fulfills their functions with the aid of an apparatus for the automatic
            registering of touches; the Referee may also be assisted by two judges watching for the
            use of the unarmed hand or arm, substitution of the valid target, touches scored on the
            floor in épée, leaving the strip to the side or the rear or any other offense defined in the
            Rules (cf. t.170).
       2.   The judges are obligatory for all individual competition finals (whether of 4 or 8 fencers)
            and for the final (2 teams) of team events.
       3.   The judges are placed on each side of the Referee, on either side of the strip; they watch
            all aspects of the entire bout.
       4.   The judges must change ends halfway through each bout or after each period in direct
            elimination bouts and after each bout in team matches, so as not to watch the same fencer
            the whole time.
