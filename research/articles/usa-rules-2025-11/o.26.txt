       For Open A-Grade, Grand Prix and World Cup team competitions, 7 referees, proposed by
       the Refereeing Commission, will be designated by the Executive Committee, and delegations
       will not have to provide any referees. The additional referees required (not less than 5) will be
       provided by the organizing committee. All the referees will be at the expense of the
       organizers, who will in return keep the entry fees.
