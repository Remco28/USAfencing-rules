t.74
            For all official FIE competitions, every fencer must appear on the strip with clothing
            conforming to the rules as follows:
       1.   Name and nationality, in accordance with the Rules, on the back of the jacket
       2.   Wearing their national uniform and logo (cf. m.25.3).
             In case of violation of this rule, the referee will eliminate the fencer at fault from the
            event in question.
