t.55
       1.   The Referee will disregard touches which are registered as a result of actions:
            — started before the word “Fence” or after the word “Halt” (cf. t.23.1/3);
            — which are made on any object other than the opponent or their equipment.
       2.   A competitor who intentionally causes the apparatus to register a touch by placing their
            point on the ground or on any surface other than that of their opponent will be penalized
            as specified in Articles t.158-162, t.166, t.170, for the offenses of the 2nd group.
