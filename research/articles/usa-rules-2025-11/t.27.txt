t.27
       1.   Displacing the target and ducking are allowed even if during the action the unarmed
            hand and/or the knee of the back leg comes into contact with the strip.
       2.   It is forbidden to turn one’s back on one’s opponent during the bout. Should such an
            offense occur, the Referee will penalize the fencer at fault as specified in Articles t.158-162,
            t.165, t.170, for the offenses of the 1st group and any touch scored by the fencer at fault is
            annulled.
