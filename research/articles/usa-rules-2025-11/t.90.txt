       1.   The épée is a thrusting weapon only. Attacks with this weapon are therefore made only
            with the point.
       2.   Pushing or letting the point of the electric weapon drag on the electric strip is forbidden
            during the actual bout (between “Fence” and “Halt”). Placing the point of the weapon on
            the strip at any time to straighten it is also forbidden.
            Any breaking of this rule will be penalized according to Articles t.158-162, t.165, t.170, for
            the offenses of the 1st group.
