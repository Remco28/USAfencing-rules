t.28
       1.   When a fencer goes completely past the opponent during a bout, the Referee must
            immediately call “Halt” and replace the competitors in the positions which they occupied
            before the passing took place.
       2.   When touches are made as a fencer passes the opponent, the touch made immediately is
            valid; a touch made after passing the opponent by the competitor who has made the
            passing movement is annulled, but the touch made immediately, even when turning
            around, by the competitor who has been subjected to the offensive action, is valid.
       3.   If during a bout a fencer who has made a flèche attack has a touch registered against and
            continues to run beyond the extreme limit of the strip sufficiently far to cause the reel or
            the connecting line to the reel to be torn out, the touch which they received will not be
            annulled (cf. t.147).
