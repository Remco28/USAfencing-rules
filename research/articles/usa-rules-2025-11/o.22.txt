       1.   The organizers of World Cup events, individual and team, must ensure the presence of a
            Supervisor of the FIE from a country other than that of the organizers, whose task it will
            be to verify that the competition properly fulfills the World Cup criteria.

       2.   The Supervisor is appointed by the Executive Committee of the FIE, at the suggestion of
            the FIE Bureau.

       3.   At competitions where there is no Refereeing Commission delegate, SEMI Commission
            delegate or Medical Commission delegate, it is the Supervisor who fulfills these
            respective functions.

       4.   The Supervisor settles any other disputes that might arise in World Cup and Grand Prix
            competitions.

       5.   The travel, board and lodging costs of the supervisor are the responsibility of the
            organizers, in accordance with the standards updated from time to time by the Executive
            Committee of the FIE.
