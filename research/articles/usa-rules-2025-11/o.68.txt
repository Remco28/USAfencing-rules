       1.   The pools are composed taking account of the initial seeding in the competition, which is

            the ranking. The pools are composed using the following method:

       2.    The allocation of fencers in the pools must be made in such a way as to place fencers of
             the same nationality in different pools, as far as possible. If a fencer is placed in a pool
             where there is already a fencer of the same nationality, the fencer is moved one or more
             places down the ranking until they are placed in a pool without a fencer of the same
             nationality. If this is not possible, the fencer must remain in the original pool.
       3.    The order of fencers on the pool sheet is decided by drawing lots.
       4.    The pools must be fenced up to the last bout.
