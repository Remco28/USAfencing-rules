t.159
        Penalties are cumulative and they are valid for the bout with the exception of exclusion,
        which is indicated by a Black Card, which means exclusion from the competition, suspension
        for the remainder of the tournament and for the following 60 days of the active season (1
        September – World Championships for the Juniors, and 1 September – World Championships
        for the Seniors), whether current or forthcoming, or both (cf. t.169.2).


        In USA Fencing competitions, black cards are penalized with exclusion from the competition, but are
        not associated with automatic suspension.
