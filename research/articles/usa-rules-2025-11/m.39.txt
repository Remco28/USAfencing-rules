m.39
       1.   If material or equipment presented to the Checking Center appears to have been
            assembled in such a way that the fencer can control in a fraudulent manner the
            registering of touches or the malfunctioning of the judging apparatus, the representative
            of the SEMI Commission may, after the examination of the irregular items, require a
            penalty against the person who submitted them.
       2.   The fencers or the team captain can only insist on the return of the equipment which has
            been checked one hour before the start of the event.
       3.   Any repairs to equipment rejected during the checking can be carried out in the repair
            workshop. Repaired equipment will, however, only be tested again after the first set of
            checks to fencers’ equipment has been completed.
