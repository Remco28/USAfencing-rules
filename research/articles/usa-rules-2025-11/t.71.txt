       If a fencer appears on the strip:
       — with only one regulation weapon conforming with the Rules (cf. t.144, t.115); or
       — with only one regulation bodycord; or
       — with only one regulation mask cord; or
       — with a weapon or a bodycord which does not work or which does not conform with the
        Rules; or
       — without a protective under-plastron (cf. t.64.5 above); or
       — with a conductive jacket which does not fully cover the valid target; or
       — with clothing which does not conform with the Rules;
       the Referee will apply the penalties according to Articles t.158-162, t.165, t.170, for the
         offenses of the 1st group.
