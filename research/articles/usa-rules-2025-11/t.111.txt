        The Referee and/or the Bout Committee, on their own authority or at the request of an official
        delegate of the FIE or of the Organizing Committee, can decide to expel from the competition
        venue, with or without a warning, any person who by gestures, attitude or language or by
        breaching the FIE Safeguarding Policy disturbs the good order or smooth running of the event.
