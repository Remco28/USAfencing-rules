t.135
        The disciplinary authorities are the following:
        —      the Referee (cf. t.137);
        —      the Refereeing Commission delegate(s);
        —      the Bout Committee (cf. t.139, o.15-o.22);
        —      the Head Referee (cf. t.138);
        —      the Executive Committee of the IOC at the Olympic Games (cf. t.142);
        —      the Bureau of the F.I.E. (cf. t.143.1/4, o.12);
        —      the Executive Committee of the F.I.E. (cf. t.143.5);
        —      the Disciplinary Commission of the FIE and its Tribunal;
        —      the Court of Arbitration for Sport and the Sports Arbitration Tribunal.
        See also the Disciplinary Code of the FIE (Chapter VII of the FIE Statutes).
