t.70
       In whatever circumstances a fencer on the strip is found to be in possession of equipment
       which is non-regulation or defective (according to Material Rules), the referee confiscates the
       equipment and submits it to the experts on duty for examination. The equipment in question
       will only be returned to the owner after the measures necessitated by this examination have
       been completed and, if appropriate, after the payment of any expenses for repairs. The
       equipment must be rechecked before it is used again.
