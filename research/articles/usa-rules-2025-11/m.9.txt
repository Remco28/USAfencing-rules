       1.   The guard must be able to pass through a straight cylindrical gauge having a diameter of
            12 cm and a length of 15 cm, the blade being parallel with the axis of the cylinder.
       2.   Eccentric mounting is forbidden, that is to say that the blade must pass through the center
            of the guard. The diameter of the guard must be between 9.5 cm and 12 cm.
