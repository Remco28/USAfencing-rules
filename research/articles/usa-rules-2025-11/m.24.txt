       1.   The guard must be full in shape, made in one piece and externally smooth. It must have a
            convex form which is continuous, without rim or holes.
       2.   It must be able to pass through a rectangular gauge measuring 15 cm by 14 cm in section,
            with a length of 15 cm, the blade being parallel with the longitudinal axis of the gauge.
       3.   Inside the guard there must be a socket into which the bodycord is plugged, whatever
            system is used.
       4.   The two sockets of the bodycord plug must be in direct contact with the body of the
            guard, making a closed electrical circuit through the bodycord, the reel and the cable
            connecting the reel to the scoring apparatus.
       5.   The resistance in the weapon must not exceed 1 Ω.
       6.   The interior of the guard must be completely insulated by means of insulating paint or a
            pad.
       7.   The exterior of the guard must be insulated for 7–8 cm from the pommel.
       8.   The grip and the pommel must be completely insulated.
