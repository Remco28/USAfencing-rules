t.147
        Although a competitor may in fact have touched their opponent on the target, this touch may
        be disallowed, either because it did not arrive during the period of time during which fencing
        is allowed, or because the competitor had crossed the boundaries of the strip, or because of
        defects in the electrical equipment, or because violence was involved in the making of the
        touch, or because of other reasons as laid down in the Rules (according to the Technical Rules).
        Award of a touch which has not in fact been received
