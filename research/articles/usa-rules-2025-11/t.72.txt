t.72
       When during a bout an irregularity is found in the equipment which could be caused by
       conditions during the bout, for example
       — conductive jacket with holes in which touches are registered as non-valid;
       — weapon or bodycord no longer functioning;
       — pressure of the spring in the point too weak;
       — the travel in the point no longer regulation;
       the Referee will apply neither warning nor penalty and any touch scored with the equipment
       that has become defective will be awarded.
       However, even during the course of a bout, any fencer whose weapon, at the moment the
       fencer presents themself on guard and ready to fence, has a curve of the blade, which exceeds
       that permitted (cf. m.8.6, m.16.2, m.23.4) commits an offense in the 1 st group and will be
       penalized in accordance with Articles t.158-162, t.166, t.170.


       In FIE competitions, even during the course of a bout, any fencer whose mask, at the moment they
       present on guard ready to fence, is not securely held on their head by the 2nd security device, commits an
       offense in the first groups and will be penalized in accordance with t.158-162, t.165, t.170.
