t.115
        1.   When presenting themselves to fence a bout, the fencers must arrive on the strip
             completely ready to fence—regulation clothing, jacket fastened, sword-hand gloved and
             holding the weapon, bodycord connected to the plug inside the guard. The mask should
             be carried in the unarmed hand.
        2.   Before the start of the bout, the fencers’ hair must be fastened and placed inside the
             clothing and/or mask in such a way as to ensure that:
             — It does not cover a valid surface (and thus prevent a touch from being scored);
             — It does not conceal the name and nationality of the fencer;
             — It does not need to be put back in place during the bout, thus interrupting it.
