        The penalties are as follows:
        1.    a warning, indicated by a Yellow Card with which the Referee identifies the fencer at
              fault. The fencer then knows that any further offense on their part will result in a penalty
              touch.
        2.    a penalty touch, indicated by a Red Card with which the Referee identifies the fencer at
              fault. A touch is added to the score of their opponent and may, if the last touch is at stake,
              lead to the loss of the bout. Furthermore, a Red Card can only be followed by another Red
              Card or by a Black Card, depending on the nature of the second offense (cf. t.170).
        3.    exclusion, from the competition or the whole tournament as applicable (for a fencer),
              indicated by a Black Card by which the Referee identifies the person at fault.
