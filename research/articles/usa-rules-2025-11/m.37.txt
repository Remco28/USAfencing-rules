       1.   In all official FIE competitions the fencers are responsible for their equipment (including
            weapons and clothes) at the moment they present themselves on the strip.
       2.   In particular, blades, masks and clothing must all carry the label of guarantee specified in
            the safety standards appended to these Rules.
       3.   The forms of checking laid down by these Rules are only intended to help organizers
            who must apply the Rules and fencers who must always respect these Rules. These checks
            can, therefore, in no way absolve any fencers who break the Rules from responsibility.
