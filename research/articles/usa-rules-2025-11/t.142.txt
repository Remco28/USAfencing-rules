USA Fencing Rules for Competition                    55                               Version: November 2025
         The Executive Committee of the International Olympic Committee (IOC) is the final arbiter
        for all disputes of a non-technical nature which may arise during the Olympic Games. It may
        intervene either on its own responsibility, or at the request of a national Olympic Committee,
        the FIE or the Organizing Committee.
        USA Fencing - National Office, Disciplinary Commission, Executive Committee
