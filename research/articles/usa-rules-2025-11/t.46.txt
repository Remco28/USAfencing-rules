t.46
       1.   By accepting a position as Referee or judge, the person so designated pledges their honor
            to respect the Rules and to cause them to be respected, and to carry out their duties with
            the strictest impartiality and absolute concentration.
       2.   Referees may not combine their function with any other activity during the tournament,
            such as member of the Bout Committee, team captain, official delegate of their national
            federation, coach, etc.
