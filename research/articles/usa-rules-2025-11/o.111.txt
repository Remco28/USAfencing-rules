The Directoire Technique is composed of people who have the experience and competence to
organize competitions.
The Directoire Technique is composed of four members of different nationalities, one of whom must
represent the country where the competition is held and, if possible, a representative of the country
where the competition will be held the following year. There must be at least one member from each
gender.
