t.123

        During or after a bout, even if the fencer has already left the strip, any act against the spirit of
        sportsmanship such as violently or dangerously throwing one’s mask (or any other piece of
        equipment) will be penalized as specified in Article t.169 (cf. t.108.1, 2 and t.109), for the
        offenses of the 4th group.

        Unwillingness to Fence (Non-combativity)
