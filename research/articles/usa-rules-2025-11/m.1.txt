m.1
      1.   There are three types of weapon: foil, épée and sabre.
      2.   All weapons are authorized providing only that they conform to these regulations and to
           the safety standards which are attached.
      3.   The weapon should be so constructed that it cannot normally injure either the user or
           their opponent. All methods of treating a blade between the guard and the tip (point), by
           grinding, filing or other methods, are forbidden.
      4.   Sharpening the edges or angles of the point is forbidden.
