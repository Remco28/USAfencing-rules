m.18
       1.   The épée has two electric wires, glued in a groove in the blade, which connect the point to
            two of the three sockets situated inside the guard and which form the active circuit of the
            épée. The body of the épée is connected to the third socket.
       2.   The handle must have, at the end which is in contact with the guard, a notch of 2 mm
            minimum depth, which allows the electric wires and their insulating sheaths to pass
            towards the sockets inside the guard without being squashed (cf. m.31.7).
