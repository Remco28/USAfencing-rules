t.152
        All participants or non-competitors who participate in or are present at a competition
        (instructors, coaches, technicians, supporters, officials, spectators) may be expelled. Such
        expulsion has the effect of forbidding them access to the venue for the duration of the
        competition or tournament (cf. t.133, t.137.3, t.170). In no circumstances can the imposition of
        this penalty give cause for redress to anyone.
        Disqualification
