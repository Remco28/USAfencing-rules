t.23
       1.   The start of the bout is signaled by the word “Fence.” No movement made or initiated
            before the word “Fence” is counted.
       2.   The bout stops on the word “Halt,” except in the case of special events occurring which
            modify the regular and normal conditions of the bout (cf. t.44.1/2).
       3.   As soon as the order “Halt” has been given, a competitor may not start a new action; only
            the movement which has begun before the order was given remains valid. Everything
            which takes place afterwards is entirely non-valid (But cf. t.44.1/2).
       4.   If a competitor stops before the word “Halt,” and is touched, the touch is valid.
       5.   The order “Halt” is also given if the fencing of the competitors is dangerous, confused, or
            contrary to the Rules, if one of the competitors is disarmed or leaves the strip (cf. t.33,
            t.58).
       6.   The Referee may not allow a fencer to leave the strip, save in exceptional circumstances. If
            a competitor does so without permission, the competitor is liable to incur the penalties
            enumerated in Articles t.158-162, t.165, t.170, for the offenses in the 1st group.
