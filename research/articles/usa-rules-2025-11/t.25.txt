       1.   Corps à corps is said to exist when the two competitors are in contact; when this occurs
            the Referee must stop the bout (cf. t.32, t.26).

USA Fencing Rules for Competition                   12                              Version: November 2025
       2.   In all three weapons it is forbidden for a fencer to cause corps à corps intentionally to
            avoid being touched, or to jostle the opponent. Should such an offense occur, the Referee
            will penalize the fencer at fault as specified in Articles t.158-162, t.165, t.170 for the
            offenses in the 1st group and any touch scored by the fencer at fault is annulled.
       3.   A fencer who either by a flèche attack or by advancing vigorously brings about a corps à
            corps even several times in succession (without brutality or violence) does not transgress
            the basic conventions of fencing and commits no fault thereby (cf. t.32).
