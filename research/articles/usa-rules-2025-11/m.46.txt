m.46
       1.   Judging apparatus for use in a World Championship or the Olympic Games must have
            been approved by the SEMI Commission of the FIE.
       2.   In order to obtain this approval, the apparatus which it is proposed to use must be
            submitted complete, with reels, connections, etc., for an examination by the said
            Commission, at a place and on a date to be agreed with the Commission, at least six
            months before the date of the competition.
       3.   The apparatus must be accompanied by a detailed drawing showing its construction.
