o.21
       1.   The functions of the medical delegates include the strict and complete organization of the
            medical aspect. They have the obligation to see that the Rules are adhered to and cannot
            themselves decide on any departure from the Rules except when circumstances arise in
            which it is absolutely impossible to apply them.
       2.   Hence, medical delegates:
            a) Verify the medical services and oversee their operation;
            b) Supervise the anti-doping control;
            c) Oversee the evaluation and treatment of an injury or cramp in accordance with t.45.
