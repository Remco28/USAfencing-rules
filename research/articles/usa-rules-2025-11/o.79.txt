o.79
       1.   At any stage of the competition, if for whatever reason, a fencer does not fence, or is
            unable to fence, or does not complete a bout, their opponent is declared winner of that
            bout. A fencer who withdraws does not lose their place in the overall classification of the
            competition and is recorded in the result list with the information “Did Not Finish.”
       2.   A fencer who is excluded is scratched from the direct elimination table and their results
            are annulled as if they had not taken part. The fencer is recorded without a classification at
            the end of the final classification list with the information “Excluded.” Cf. t.124.3.b, t.170.
