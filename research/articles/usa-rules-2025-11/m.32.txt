m.32
       1.   The metal mesh of the mask must not be insulated and must ensure electrical
            conductivity.
       2.   The bib and any trim must be entirely covered with conductive material with the same
            electrical characteristics as the conductive jacket.
       3.   The trim may also be made of conductive material.
       4.   The electrical contact between the conductive jacket and the mask must be ensured by
            means of a wire and one or two crocodile clips. The wire must be attached, either by
            means of a crocodile clip or by being soldered, to the mesh of the mask, and must be
            between 30 cm and 40 cm long. In the case of a coiled cable, the maximum length of the
            free cable must not exceed 25 cm in length, with a tolerance of ± 5 cm.
       5.   The electrical resistance between the crocodile clip and any point on the mask must be
            less than 5 Ω. The crocodile clip, the design and size of which must conform with the
            conditions laid down in Article m.29, must be soldered to the other end of the wire. In
            addition, the electrical resistance in this wire (between crocodile clip and crocodile clip or
            crocodile clip and soldering) must not exceed 1 Ω. The wire must be white colored or
            clear.


       At USA Fencing tournaments, there is no restriction on the color of the mask cord. It may be any color.
