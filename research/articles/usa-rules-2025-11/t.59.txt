t.59
       1.   As soon as the bout has stopped, the Referee briefly reconstructs the movements which
            composed the last fencing phrase.
       2.   After reaching a decision regarding the materiality of a touch, the Referee, by applying
            the rules, decides which fencer was touched, whether both were touched (épée) or if there
            was no valid touch (cf. t.82ss, t.92, t.100).
       Appeals for Video Refereeing


       Rules t.60-62 only applies to USA Fencing tournaments where video refereeing is in use.
