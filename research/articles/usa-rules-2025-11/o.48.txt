       1.   Any proposed candidature for organizing the World Championships must be studied at
            the venue concerned by an ad hoc delegation designated by the Executive Committee, at
            the invitation of the candidate federation.
       2.   The organizing committee of the World Championships, who will receive all the entry
            fees from the participating delegations, must, at their own expense, invite the following
            international officials (tourist-class return air fare, accommodation and daily allowances):
            a)   The President of the FIE or their representative, who presides over the World
                 Championships and, in particular, controls the smooth running of the Bout
                 Committee.
            b)   Eight members of the Bout Committee appointed by the Executive Committee of the
                 FIE, of whom one must belong to the organizing country and one is Head of Protocol.
            c)   Three members of the SEMI Commission, appointed by the Executive Committee of
                 the FIE.
            d)   Six members of the Refereeing Commission, one of whom is designated principal
                 delegate, appointed by the Executive Committee of the FIE.
            e)   Three members of the Medical Commission, appointed by the Executive Committee
                 of the FIE.
            f)   The referees designated by the Executive Committee at the proposal of the Refereeing
                 Commission in accordance with the Handbook of Regulations.
