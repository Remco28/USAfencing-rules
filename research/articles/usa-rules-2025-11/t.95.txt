       The Referee must also apply the following rules regarding the annulment of touches:

       1.   If the incidents mentioned in Article t.94 occur as a result of the competitor’s bodycord
            being unplugged (either near the hand or at the back of the fencer), they cannot justify the
            annulment of the touch registered.

            However, if the safety device prescribed by Article m.55.4 is missing or not functioning,
            the touch should be annulled if the plug at the fencers’ back has become unplugged.

       2.   If a competitor tears the conductive strip by a touch made on the ground and, at the same
            time, the apparatus registers a touch against their opponent, the touch must be annulled.
