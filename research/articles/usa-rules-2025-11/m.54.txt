m.54
       1.   Before every World Championship or Olympic Games, a delegate of the SEMI
            Commission must check the proper working of the apparatus to be used and that they
            conform to the type registered. This is independent of the registration of the type of
            apparatus mentioned above (cf. m.52).
       2.   The apparatus must be made available to this delegate at least 48 hours before the
            beginning of the competition.
