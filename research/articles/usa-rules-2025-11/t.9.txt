t.9
       1.   The offensive actions are the attack, the riposte and the counter-riposte.
            —    The attack is the initial offensive action made by extending the arm and continuously
                 threatening the opponent’s target, preceding the launching of the lunge or flèche (cf.
                 t.56ss, t.75ss).
            —    The riposte is the offensive action made by the fencer who has parried the attack.
            —    The counter-riposte is the offensive action made by the fencer who has parried the
                 riposte.
       2.   The defensive actions are the parries.
            —    The parry is the defensive action made with the weapon to prevent an offensive
                 action arriving.
