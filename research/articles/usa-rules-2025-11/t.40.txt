       1.   The bout ends when:
            —    One of the fencers has scored 15 touches; or
            —    9 minutes of effective fencing time have passed.
       2.   The fencer who has scored the greater number of touches is declared the winner.

       3.   If at the end of regulation time the scores are equal, the fencers fence for a deciding
            touch, with a maximum time limit of one minute. Before the fencing recommences the
            Referee draws lots to decide who will be the winner if scores are still equal at the end of
            the extra minute.


       In épée, during this minute, when double touches occur, points will not be awarded, the score will not
       change, and the fencers will retain their respective positions on the strip.


       4.   In this case the score recorded on the scoresheet is the real score achieved in the bout.
