t.1
      In competition, the fencer’s salute is a gesture of civility toward the opponent, the referee and the
      spectators. To perform the fencer’s salute, the two fencers:

      -   face each other
      -   stand still on their on-guard line
      -   place the mask under the non-sword are
      -   lift the guard to the chin
      -   then lower the blade to the ground
