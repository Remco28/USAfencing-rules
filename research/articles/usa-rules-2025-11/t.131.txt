t.131

        1.   During the direct elimination of the individual events, the fencer’s coach, medical staff
             and technicians are not allowed to remain near their fencers; the coaches are allowed to be
             inside the competition area in a place indicated by the organizers.

        In USA Fencing individual competitions, only competitors may be inside the Strip Enclosure during the
        pool round. During individual direct elimination rounds, each competitor may designate one person to
        be inside the Strip Enclosure. This person must stay in the designated area at their fencer’s end of the
        strip.


        2.   The Referee may, whenever they consider it necessary, authorize a person to come briefly
             to the assistance of a fencer.
        3.   Each nation which has a fencer taking part in the round of the competition in progress
             may designate a maximum of two people who have the right to be positioned near the
             Strip Enclosure, outside it but close to a point of access. The organizers must provide the
             necessary space for these people.
