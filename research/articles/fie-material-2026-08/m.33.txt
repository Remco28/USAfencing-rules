      2. The conductive material must be turned over into the inside of the gauntlet to a
         depth of at least 5 cm.

      3. In order to guarantee a good contact with the sleeve of the conductive jacket, it is
         necessary to use an elastic band, a popper button or any system which will
         guarantee conductivity and which has been approved by the SEMI Committee.
         When a conductive overlay is worn, the overlay must contain a device which fixes
         the position of the overlay on the arm so that its position on the arm cannot be
         changed during the bout.
