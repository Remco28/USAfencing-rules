m.59. 1. a)   Extension lamps outside the apparatus are obligatory for the official
              competitions of the FIE.

           b) The lamps should be placed at a minimum of 1.80 m above the piste. (When
              the fencers fence on a 0.50 m high podium, the extension lamps must be 2.30
              m above ground.)

           c) The extension lamps signalling valid hits are coloured, one red, the other
              green, and they must be of 2600 lumens minimum.

      2. a) The white lights indicating non-valid hits need be of only 1100 lumens.
           b) The two lamps of the same group must not be further apart than 15 cm.
      3. The white and coloured extension lamps must be arranged on either side of the
         apparatus, either horizontally or vertically, and must be visible from all directions
         (cf. Annexe B, paragraph A.1.(a).2).
