1.    The items of equipment which have been thus checked will be distinctively marked.
      A fencer must not, on pain of penalties (cf. t.120), use any equipment which does not
      bear this check mark.
2.   Any repairs to equipment rejected during the checking can be carried out in the repair
workshop. Repaired equipment will, however, only be tested again after the first set of
checks of the other fencers’ equipment has been completed.
