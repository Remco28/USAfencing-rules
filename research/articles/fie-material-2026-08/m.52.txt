         immediately in order to obtain the names of manufacturers whose electrical
         apparatus is accepted by the Committee.

      3. The Organising Committee usually prefers combined apparatus, which can be used
         for all three weapons. The Organising Committee must choose a manufacturer who
         will provide good quality equipment for the smooth technical running of
         competitions; this equipment must be approved by the SEMI Committee.
