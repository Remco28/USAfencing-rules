m.32. 1. The metal mesh of the mask must not be insulated and must ensure electrical
          conductivity.

      2. The bib and any trim must be entirely covered with conductive material with the
         same electrical characteristics as the conductive jacket.

      3. The trim may also be made of conductive material.

      4. The electrical contact between the conductive jacket and the mask must be
         ensured by means of a white coloured or clear covered wire with two crocodile
         clips. The wire must be attached to the mask with one crocodile clip and to the
         jacket with another crocodile clip. This wire must be between 30cm and 40cm
         long. A coiled mask wire is not allowed.

      5. The electrical resistance between the crocodile clip and any point on the mask
         must be less than 5 ohms. The crocodile clips, the design and size of which must
