m.2. All weapons are composed of the following parts.

      1. A flexible steel blade completed at its forward extremity by a button and at the
         rear by the tang (the latter included in the handle when the weapon is mounted).

          At all three weapons, the use of maraging steel blades is obligatory.

      2. A handle within which the tang of the blade is fixed, and which enables the
         fencer’s hand to hold the weapon. It may be composed of one or several parts:
         in the latter case it is divided into a grip (which is normally held in the hand)
         and a pommel (rear portion of the handle which locks the handle onto the
         tang).

      3. A metal guard fixed (with the convex face towards the front) between the
         blade and the handle, serving to protect the sword hand. For foil and épée,
         the guard must contain a padding or cushion (cf. m.5/2) to reduce the effect
         of blows. It will also contain a socket to which the bodywire can be
         connected.

      Dimensions (cf. m.7ss, m.15ss, m.21ss)
