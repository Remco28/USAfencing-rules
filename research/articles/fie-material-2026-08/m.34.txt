m.34. 1. The fencer must wear, over his jacket, a conductive over-jacket, the conductive
         surface of which must cover entirely and without omission the valid surface of the
         body above a horizontal line which, when the fencer is on guard, joins, round the
         fencer’s trunk, the tops of the two hip bones.

      2. The conductive surface must cover the arms as far as the wrists. The jacket must
         have a collar which is at least 3 cm high. The jacket must have a conductive flap,
         minimum 2 cm x 3 cm in the middle of the back, just below the collar, to which the
         crocodile clip from the mask can be attached.

      3. Whatever means of fastening is used, the conductive material must be ample
         enough to guarantee covering the valid target area in any position.

      4. The conductive material (lamé) must satisfy the conditions laid down for testing
         (cf. m.28).

      5. The sleeves of the conductive jacket must be fixed at the wrist by means of an
         elastic band. There must be a strap passing between the fencer’s legs to keep the
         jacket in place (see Figure 15).
