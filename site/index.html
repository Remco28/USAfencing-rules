Source and scope

November 2025 chart · October 2026 updates

This unofficial study aid is based on USA Fencing’s November 2025 Rules for Competition and penalty chart. Passivity (t.124), mask safety (t.72) and warm-up guidance (t.20) include the USA Fencing updates effective October 1, 2026. Unchanged chart rows and excerpts retain their November 2025 source date. The official rulebook and current updates take precedence.
